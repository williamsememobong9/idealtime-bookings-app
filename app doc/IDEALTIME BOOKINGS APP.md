# **IDEALTIME BOOKINGS APP**

## **Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Version 2.0**

### **1\. Product Overview**

**Product Name:** Idealtime Bookings App

**Product Category:** Accommodation Marketplace & Stay Management Platform

**Primary Market:** Nigeria, with potential expansion across Africa

**Product Positioning:**

> **Find. Verify. Book. Stay.**

Idealtime Bookings is a trusted accommodation marketplace that connects customers with verified hotels, serviced apartments, short-let apartments and other accommodation providers.

The platform will allow customers to discover, compare, verify, book and pay for accommodation while providing property owners, managers and agents with tools to manage listings, availability, bookings, guests and revenue.

The product will go beyond accommodation booking by supporting transportation, airport transfers, corporate accommodation, cleaning, maintenance, event and holiday services and other services connected to a customer's stay.

---

# **2\. Vision**

To become a trusted Nigerian accommodation and stay-management platform where customers can confidently find and book quality accommodation while property owners and managers can efficiently manage their properties and bookings.

### **Vision Statement**

> **To make accommodation booking in Nigeria more transparent, trustworthy, convenient and professionally managed.**

---

# **3\. Mission**

Idealtime Bookings will solve common problems associated with accommodation booking, including:

* Fake property listings  
* Fake agents  
* Outdated availability  
* Misleading property pictures  
* Unexpected charges  
* Poor communication  
* Unverified guests  
* Booking cancellations  
* Difficulty managing shortlet properties  
* Lack of professional corporate accommodation management  
* Poor support when problems occur during a stay

---

# **4\. User Groups**

The platform has two user groups only.

## **A. Guests**

People looking for:

* Hotels  
* Serviced apartments  
* Shortlet apartments  
* Vacation accommodation  
* Business accommodation  
* Family accommodation  
* Extended stays  
* Relocation accommodation  
* Event accommodation

### **Guest segments**

* Business travellers  
* Families  
* Couples  
* Tourists  
* Returning Nigerians  
* Expatriates  
* Contractors  
* Students  
* NYSC participants  
* Corporate employees (book as guests under a business profile)  
* Relocating individuals  
* Event attendees

Anyone booking on behalf of someone else (including agents booking for clients) acts as a guest for that booking.

---

## **B. Hosts**

Individuals or companies that list accommodation and receive bookings. Host subtypes share the same listing, availability, booking, guest, cleaning, maintenance, and revenue tools. They differ only by verification and permissions:

* **Owner-hosts:** list own properties. Can list, receive bookings, manage availability and pricing, communicate with guests, track revenue, receive payments, collect reviews.
* **Manager-hosts:** manage properties for owners. Additionally manage multiple properties and calendars, guests, cleaners, maintenance, and reports.
* **Agent-hosts:** verified agents listing properties they are authorized to represent. Additionally manage inquiries and clients, make bookings for clients, track commissions, maintain a professional profile.

Companies needing accommodation for staff (employees, executives, consultants, contractors, visitors, project teams) book as guests, with dedicated business profiles and billing where applicable.
